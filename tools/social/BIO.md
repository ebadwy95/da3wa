# الحساب على إنستقرام

## البايو

```
💌 دعوات إلكترونية لكل مناسباتكم
🤍 من أول معزوم وإحنا معاكم
📍 الكويت · احجزوا من الرابط
```

Copy the three lines exactly, line breaks included. Instagram keeps line breaks
when they are pasted in; typing Enter inside the app's bio field sometimes eats
them, so paste rather than retype.

**Name field** (the searchable one, separate from the @handle):

```
دعوة | Da3wa
```

The name field is indexed by Instagram's search and the handle is not enough on
its own — someone who hears the name spoken will type it in Arabic.

## Why this wording

Eslam asked for something you can put your hand on, not something technical.
The order matters as much as the words:

**Line one says what it is.** Somebody landing here from a story has about a
second to work out whether this is for them. "دعوات إلكترونية لكل مناسباتكم" —
لكل مناسباتكم, not للأعراس: weddings pay the bills, but graduations, engagements
and openings all buy the same thing, and a bio that says weddings turns them
away before they ask.

**Line two says what it is like to work with us.** "من أول معزوم وإحنا معاكم"
is the whole service in five words, and it is a promise about people rather
than about software. This is the line that had to stop sounding like a product.

**Line three says where and what to do.** Kuwait, because a Gulf customer wants
to know you are in their country before anything else, and one instruction.

## What is deliberately not in it

No "منصة", no "نظام", no "QR", no "أونلاين". Every one of those describes the
machinery, and nobody books a wedding service because of the machinery. The
scanner on the door is the strongest thing we have to sell and it still does
not belong here — it belongs in a story where people can see it working.

No hashtags either. In a bio they are not searchable, they just spend lines.

## Still to do, and only from the phone

Both of these need the Instagram app; neither can be done from a browser.

1. **Add the link.** Settings → Edit profile → Links → Add external link, and
   paste `https://www.da3wa.digital`. The line above says "احجزوا من الرابط"
   and reads as a mistake until the link is actually there.
2. **Switch to a Business account.** Settings → Account type and tools →
   Switch to professional account → Business, category "Wedding Planning
   Service". This is also what the WhatsApp Business API needs the account
   connected as.
