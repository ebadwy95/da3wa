# Where the invitation music comes from

Eslam asked for a real recorded piece rather than the oud loop the first
version used — soft, instrumental, and something a couple would actually want
under their names.

Every link below was opened and checked on 2026-08-29. That check is the point
of this file: **freepd.com is closed** (it shut down in 2025 after 17 years and
now serves nothing but a closure notice), and it is still the first result
recommended almost everywhere. Anything added here should be opened first.

## Where to look

| Library | Licence in one line | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/) | Pixabay Content Licence — free, commercial use, **no attribution required** | The default. Largest soft-piano and cinematic selection with nothing to sign. |
| [Chosic — wedding](https://www.chosic.com/free-music/wedding/) | Mixed; each track states CC0 / CC-BY / Pixabay | A curated wedding shelf. Read the badge on the track, not the page. |
| [Mixkit — wedding](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/wedding/) | Mixkit Free Licence — free, commercial use, no attribution | Short, well-mastered pieces. Small catalogue, quick to skim. |
| [Uppbeat — wedding](https://uppbeat.io/browse/music/wedding) | Free tier **requires credit**; paid tier removes it | Best-produced of the free options. The credit line is a real condition. |
| [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/genre/Instrumental/) | Per-track CC — some CC-BY, some non-commercial | Deep catalogue, but check each licence. Non-commercial tracks are not usable here. |
| [Incompetech](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/music.html) | CC-BY — attribution required | Kevin MacLeod's library. Well known, so it can sound generic. |

## What to pick

Solo piano or strings, no percussion, no build. The clip runs about eleven
seconds under a name — anything with a drop in it will arrive in the middle of
the couple's names. Start on a sustained note rather than a downbeat: the audio
begins on the guest's tap, and a track that opens on a hit sounds like a
mistake.

Avoid anything with vocals. The invitation is read, and a lyric competes with
the line the guest is reading.

## Installing one

Drop the file at `public/samples/music.mp3` — that is what an event's
`inviteAudioUrl` points at by default. Keep it under about 1.5MB: it is
fetched on a phone, often over mobile data, before the guest has seen anything.

If the track requires attribution (Uppbeat's free tier, Incompetech, CC-BY on
FMA), the credit belongs in this file **and** somewhere a person can reach —
attribution that only exists in a commit message is not attribution.
